METHODS
Post-discovery recalibration was evaluated prospectively under a frozen contract. The only modified retention criterion was the lower-tail stability threshold (q10 aligned correlation, 0.40 to 0.60); all other retention criteria were unchanged. The single candidate was evaluated on 400 independently seeded fresh datasets (200 correlated-private null stress datasets and 200 true-shared STANDARD datasets), with zero simulation-seed overlap with prior frozen banks. The prespecified confirmation rule required an exact paired specificity improvement at p<0.05 in the required direction, zero reverse discordance under the nested rules, and candidate STANDARD biological recovery of at least 0.80. No further threshold retuning was permitted after fresh-result unblinding.

RESULTS
In the fresh correlated-private null stress set, false acceptance decreased from 66/200 (33.0%) under q10=0.40 to 38/200 (19.0%) under q10=0.60. The paired absolute reduction was 14.0 percentage points (95% bootstrap CI 9.5 to 19.0 percentage points), with 28 rescued datasets, 0 reverse-discordant datasets, and an exact paired two-sided McNemar/binomial p value of 7.45e-09. In the true-shared safety set, biological recovery changed from 755/800 (94.375%) to 752/800 (94.000%), with 3 lost and 0 gained truth-factor opportunities. Candidate recovery remained above the prespecified 0.80 safety floor. The prospective q10=0.60 recalibration therefore met all prespecified confirmation criteria. This prospective recalibration does not alter the historical formal 05f3e gate result.

DISCUSSION / INTERPRETATION
The prospective result supports q10=0.60 as a separately validated post-discovery recalibration for the identified correlated-private specificity failure mode. The improvement was obtained without material loss of true-shared recovery in the prespecified safety condition. The original 05f3e formal gate remains unchanged and should be reported as the historical frozen result; the 05y3-05y6 sequence should be described explicitly as post-discovery recalibration followed by independent prospective confirmation.
